Privacy Policy — KeyVault

Last Updated: January 2026

KeyVault ("we", "our", or "us") operates a digital marketplace that provides users with access to digital products including game keys, gift cards, software licenses, crypto vouchers, and subscription services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including your email address, username, wallet information, and transaction details when you register an account, place an order, or contact support. We also collect technical data such as IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and usage data for security and analytics purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide, maintain, and improve our services, process orders, verify transactions, prevent fraud, communicate with you, provide customer support, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Cookies and Tracking

KeyVault uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience, remember preferences, enable secure sessions, and analyze platform usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

4. Digital Keys, Wallets, and Payments

When you purchase digital products or fund your wallet, we process transaction data securely. We do not store full payment credentials. Crypto payments are handled via secure third-party blockchain and payment service providers.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services such as payment processors, analytics tools, and communication platforms. These providers are authorized to use your data only as necessary to perform services for KeyVault.

6. Data Security

We implement technical and organizational security measures including encryption, access control, and monitoring to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

7. User Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information. You may also request a copy of your data or close your account at any time by contacting support.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed and stored in different countries. We ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data across borders.

9. Children's Privacy

KeyVault does not knowingly collect information from individuals under the age of 18. If we discover that a minor has provided personal data, we will delete it promptly.

10. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact KeyVault support through the website.
